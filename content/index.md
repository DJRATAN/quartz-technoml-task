## Purpose

[[TechnoML]] is committed to creating an inclusive, friendly, and safe environment for everyone involved in our [[Community]].  
We welcome contributors from all backgrounds — regardless of gender, identity, age, ability, ethnicity, socioeconomic status, or religion.  

Our goal is to ensure that all [[Contributors]], [[Users]], and [[Partners]] can participate and collaborate positively across all [[Projects]].

---

## Expected Behavior

All members of the [[TechnoML Community]] are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in communication and actions.  
- Contribute positively and collaborate whenever possible.  
- Avoid demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior.  
- Maintain professionalism in all public and private communications related to [[TechnoML]] and [[SRsaathi]].  

See also → [[Community Guidelines]] | [[Contributor Handbook]]

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are **not acceptable** within the [[TechnoML]] or [[SRsaathi]] community:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind.  
- Threats or acts of violence.  
- Sharing private information (“[[Doxing]]”) without consent.  
- Offensive or inappropriate content.  
- Sustained disruption or trolling during discussions or meetings.  

Learn more: [[Incident Reporting Policy]] | [[Community Moderation]]

---

## Consequences

Unacceptable behavior from any community member will not be tolerated.  
Organizers may take action such as:

- Formal warning  
- Temporary suspension  
- Permanent removal from [[TechnoML]] activities  

Reference: [[Enforcement Policy]]

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the [[TechnoML Team]]:  

- 📩 Official form → [technoml.in/contact](https://technoml.in/contact)  
- 📧 Email → **[service@technoml.in](mailto:service@technoml.in)**  

All reports will be reviewed and handled **confidentially** and **with care**.  

You can also connect via [[SRsaathi Support]] or [[Community Safety]] teams.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all [[TechnoML]] online spaces, [[Events]], [[Projects]], and all associated communication channels including [[Discord]], [[Slack]], and [[GitHub]].
